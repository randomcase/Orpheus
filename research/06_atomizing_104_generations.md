# Atomizing a Story Told Across 104 Human Generations (565 BCE–2026 CE)

## 1. The 104-Person Room

Between the carved limestone metope of **`ΟΡΦΑΣ`** at Delphi (`c. 565 BCE`) and modern retellings in **`2026 CE`** lie **`2,591` years**. Measured at **`25` years per generational hand-off**, that span is not an eternity—it is an exact chain of **`104` human generations** (`G-001` through `G-104`).

Picture all `104` generations standing shoulder-to-shoulder passing a live story hand-to-hand:

| Generational Window | Dates | Composition of the Relay | What Happens to the Story |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`G-001` to `G-021`** | `565 BCE` – `41 BCE` | Hellenic Archaic & Hellenistic chain | Orpheus is an **Argonaut, cosmologist, and underworld initiate**. Eurydice is unnamed until `G-019`, and **nobody has heard Virgil's backward glance**. |
| **`G-022` to `G-081`** | `40 BCE` – `1459 CE` | Parallel Western (`latin_monastic`) & Eastern (`greek_byzantine`) branches | Two redundant survival channels emerge: Western Latin monks preserve **Eurydice's tragic look-back**, while Byzantine codices preserve **Greek Hymns & Patristic Logos** theology. |
| **`G-082` to `G-098`** | `1460 CE` – `1884 CE` | Vernacular & print confluence | Émigré Byzantine Greek codices merge with Latin monastic Virgil/Ovid in Florence (`1462–1480`), exploding into **print editions and modern opera**. |
| **`G-099` to `G-104`** | `1885 CE` – `2026 CE` | Global stage + archaeological **Time-Capsule Tunneling** | Excavations at Delphi (`1894`), Olbia (`1951`), Derveni (`1962`), and Hipponion (`1969`) jump straight across **~90 generations** to `2026 CE`. |

## 2. The Dual-Atom Schema

A continuous 2,591-year story solves a **Ship-of-Theseus paradox** only when decomposed into two coupled atoms at every 25-year link:

1. **The Narrative Atom (Tri-State Mytheme Vector):** Only **one** invariant nucleus (`threshold_voice` — music bending boundaries) holds polarity `+1` in every generation from `565 BCE` to `2026 CE`. Seven orbital mythemes carry **tri-state polarity** (`+1` asserted, `0` uninvented/absent, `-1` deliberately subverted): `argonaut_voyage`, `mystery_passport`, `katabasis_descent`, `named_eurydice`, `taboo_c331_glance`, `sparagmos_head_oracle`, and `redemptive_return`.
2. **The Physical Carrier Atom (Survival Bottleneck):** Across `~70` "silent custodian" generations between famous poets, survival depended on **substrate durability** (`stone/gold` vs. `papyrus` vs. `parchment`), **copy redundancy**, and surviving **linguistic scripts** (Archaic Doric -> Attic -> Koine -> Byzantine Minuscule / Latin Uncial -> Modern Print & Unicode).

## 3. Dual-Stream Fault Tolerance & Deep-Time Rescue

Because Orpheus travels along **two living medieval branches** (`latin_monastic` and `greek_byzantine`) plus **four buried time capsules** (`G-001` Delphi stone, `G-004` Olbia bone, `G-007` Hipponion gold, `G-010` Derveni carbonized papyrus), breaking a single branch alters *which* Orpheus reaches `G-104` in `2026 CE`:

- **Severing Virgil (`G-022`) or Western Monasteries (`latin_monastic`):** The story still reaches `2026 CE` via Eastern Greek codices and time capsules—*without the Latin backward-glance tragedy* (`taboo_c331_glance` goes extinct).
- **Burning Every Living Manuscript After `41 BCE` (`G-021` Severed):** Western and Byzantine chains both go dark (`G-022..G-098`), yet `1894–1969` archaeology resurrects `G-001..G-010` straight into `G-099..G-104` (`capsule_resurrected: true`)—recovering the Argonaut and initiate prophet while losing `named_eurydice` and `taboo_c331_glance`.
